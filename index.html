UJN QuickBooks Bridge

UJN QuickBooks Bridge is a private tool used by UJN LLC's bookkeeper to read UJN LLC's QuickBooks Online reports and to record bookkeeping entries. It serves one company, UJN LLC, and is not offered to the public.

The connection to QuickBooks Online is made, and can be renewed, by UJN LLC's bookkeeper inside the tool's own workspace. There is nothing to sign up for on this site.
